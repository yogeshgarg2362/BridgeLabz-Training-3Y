Box-Sizing Comparison

Compare content-box and border-box.

content-box

Width: 250px
Padding: 25px
Border: 8px

Actual total width: 250 + 50 + 16 = 316px.

border-box

Width: 250px
Padding: 25px
Border: 8px

Actual total width: 250px, including padding and borders.

Explanation

With content-box, the declared width applies only to the content. Padding and borders are added outside that width.

With border-box, the declared width includes the content, padding, and borders. This makes layout sizing easier.
