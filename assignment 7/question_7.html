Why Readable Text Matters

Good typography makes information easier to understand. A thoughtful choice of font, size, and line spacing can make a page feel welcoming while helping readers follow each idea.

Use bold text to draw attention to important details, italic text to add emphasis, and highlighted text when a key phrase should stand out.

Sans-serif fonts often work well for interfaces, while serif fonts can give articles a traditional editorial feel. Comfortable line-height and restrained letter-spacing help keep paragraphs clear.

Typography supports readability and meaning.
